# Finance Module

B7 establishes the financial foundation of NEXUS:

- financial accounts and balances;
- accounts receivable created from confirmed sales;
- accounts payable created from received purchase receipts;
- partial and full payments;
- financial transactions;
- cash summary;
- tenant isolation, RBAC and audit logs.

This is an operational finance layer, not accounting/fiscal compliance. Formal accounting rules, tax documents, reconciliation and inventory valuation remain later milestones.
